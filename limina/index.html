Never say no to a new order

Documents paid for, automatically

When an HOA packet or a municipal record carries a fee, our agents pay it and file the documents the moment they arrive.

Buyers get answers without you

Questions about title get answered straight from the file, so nothing pulls you off your queue.

Realtors reached where they already are

No one reads email anymore. Limina texts agents, so an unread inbox never pushes a closing back.

Automated work, with people in charge

Limina takes on the four biggest time sinks in title processing, so you can handle more files without adding staff.

Limina works as your co-pilot. Before an abstract goes to a lender or a commitment is finalized, you review and approve. Nothing acts alone.
