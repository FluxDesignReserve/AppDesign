Why it's hard

A closing is forty small deadlines wearing one trench coat.

Every file is a relay between people who don't share a system: agents, lenders, counties, HOAs, payoff departments. Processors hold it together by reading, re-keying and reminding. That's the work Limina takes.

01

The inbox is the system of record

Contracts, CDs, payoffs and estoppels arrive by email, fax and portal — unlabeled, out of order, and often attached to the wrong thread.

02

Comparison by squint

A revised disclosure means two PDFs side by side and a hope that nothing moved. Lenders change figures without changing the filename.

03

Public records live behind logins

Tax, water and assessment data sits in county portals with no API. Someone has to log in, search, screenshot, and type it into a proration sheet.

04

Capacity is headcount

The only way to take more files has been to hire more processors — right when the market turns and you can least afford to.

How a file moves

Five jobs a processor does by hand. Limina does them before the coffee's poured.

01 · Intake

Every inbound document, filed before you open it

Drop in a purchase agreement — any standard form — and Limina reads it, opens the order, and populates the file. Everything else that arrives is matched to the order it belongs to, with a confidence score you can see.

  • Email, fax gateway and portal downloads handled the same way
  • Revised lender documents are diffed line by line — no side-by-side PDFs
  • Uncertain matches wait in a queue instead of guessing

  • Examiner notes carry forward into the commitment

03 · Coordination

The follow-ups nobody wants to send, sent at the right time

Payoff requests, lender status pings, agent updates, buyer reminders — Limina drafts each one from the file's actual state and holds it for a click, or sends it on a schedule you set. Replies are read and filed too.

  • Your voice, your templates — agents fill in the specifics
  • Threads stay attached to the order, not to someone's inbox
  • Escalates to a person the moment a reply needs judgment

Trust & control

Built for an industry where the paperwork is the product.

Closings move money and record ownership. Limina is designed so that every automated step can be inspected, attributed, and reversed.

Named approver on every action

Outbound messages, drafted documents and portal submissions carry the person who approved them and the evidence they saw.

Encrypted, segregated by shop

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and each agency's files are isolated. Nothing you upload trains a shared model.

Wire-fraud posture

Agents never change or transmit wire instructions. Payoff wires are surfaced for callback verification, never acted on.

Full-fidelity audit log

Every read, draft, edit and send is logged with timestamps and source documents, exportable for underwriter and regulator review.

Role-based permissions

Decide which action types are auto-approved, which need a processor, and which need an examiner — per client if you like.

Independent security review

Third-party penetration testing on a fixed cadence; a compliance attestation program is underway. Ask us for the current report.
